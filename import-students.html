📌 Import Rules
  • 26000–26999 → Grade 11
  • 27000–27999 → Grade 10
  • A/L students → 28Cxxxxx, 28Nxxxxx, or legacy A27000–A29999 series
  • Existing student records will not be overwritten.

🎓 A/L Student Registration
  • A/L admission numbers use the 28C + 5 digits / 28N + 5 digits (also supports legacy A27000–A29999) series.
  • Student name is not required during bulk import.
  • Temporary passwords are used for the first login.
  • Students must complete their own Full Name, NIC Number and New Password during first login.
  • Existing A/L student records will not be modified.
🔐 First Login Required: New A/L students will be redirected to the registration page after their first successful login.
