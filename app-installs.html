How this report works: A student is listed after they open the installed LMS app and successfully sign in at least once. Each Student ID is counted once, even if the student uses more than one device.
This is not a store-verified download count. Earlier installations cannot be detected retrospectively; those students will appear only after opening the installed app and signing in again. These client-reported records are usage indicators, not tamper-proof audit evidence under the current client-side database security setup.
